## About

Most of my work lives around the **Galaxy A22 5G (SM-A226B, MT6833)** and other low-end MediaTek Galaxies — from One UI ports and custom firmware to mainline Linux bring-up.

- **Custom firmware** — [H3CKED-UI](https://github.com/H3CKED-UI), a One UI ROM that brings newer features to supported low-end MediaTek devices.
- **Kernels & bring-up** — OSS kernels, GKI, KernelSU patches, and a mainline Linux port for the A22 5G.
- **Apps & modules** — Xposed/LSPosed modules, Magisk modules, and small Android apps in Kotlin.
- **Tooling** — scripts and builders that automate the boring parts of ROM, recovery and firmware work.

> **Currently:** polishing **H3CKED-UI 2.1.0**, keeping the A22 5G alive on **mainline Linux**, and shipping the occasional Xposed module.
